The Evidence

Every formula
justified by research

We aim to make research traceable and claims proportionate to the evidence. Ingredient research does not automatically establish that a particular finished product is effective. Product-specific claims should be supported by the exact formulation, dose and intended use.

How to read the evidence

We distinguish human clinical outcomes from pharmacokinetic studies, laboratory research and traditional use. A plausible mechanism is not proof of clinical benefit, and evidence for an ingredient is not automatically evidence for a finished product. Research links below open PubMed records or targeted searches so references can be checked before publication.

D3

Vitamin D3 + K2 MK-7

Dosed at 2000 IU D3 + 90mcg K2-MK7. Vitamin D contributes to normal calcium absorption and maintenance of normal bones. Claims that combining D3 and K2 prevents calcium deposition in arteries require direct clinical evidence and should not be inferred from mechanism alone.

Omega-3 EPA/DHA

1200mg EPA + 600mg DHA per serving. Molecularly distilled for purity. The clinical evidence depends on the specific omega-3 formulation and dose. REDUCE-IT studied prescription icosapent ethyl, not standard mixed EPA/DHA fish oil.
