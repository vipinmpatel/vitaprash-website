From the founder

Why I built
a supplement brand
that tells you what it won't do

Every product page on Vitaprash has a section called "What this product will not do." My team initially questioned it. Who volunteers their product's limitations?

I do. Because I'm a pharmacist. In a clinical setting, informed consent isn't optional — patients have the right to understand both what a treatment can offer and where its evidence ends. I don't see why supplements should be different.

The industry has built its entire model on overstating efficacy and understating uncertainty. Vague claims, cherry-picked studies, doses far below what the research actually used. This journal — and these products — exist to do the opposite.

"Honest health information is not a threat to a well-formulated product. It's the point."
— Founder, Vitaprash · MPharm · BAMS
