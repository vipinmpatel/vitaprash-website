Our Story

Two qualifications.
One standard.

Vitaprash was founded by a registered pharmacist and Doctor of Ayurveda — two disciplines that are rarely found in the same person, and almost never in the same supplement brand.

The name comes from Chyawanprash — the ancient Ayurvedic formulation considered one of the most sophisticated herbal preparations in traditional medicine. Vita, from the Latin for life. A bridge between two traditions.

The supplement industry has a trust problem. Proprietary blends hide ineffective doses. Claims outrun evidence. Third-party testing is the exception, not the rule. Vitaprash was built as the answer to all of that.

"What tradition knew, science has proven. What science proves, we formulate honestly."

Every product is dosed to match the quantities used in peer-reviewed clinical studies. Every batch is tested by an ISO-certified third-party laboratory. Every reference is cited. Every limitation is disclosed.

The pharmacist

MPharm · Registered

A pharmacist reads drug studies with a critical eye — examining methodology, effect sizes, funding sources, and the gap between statistical and clinical significance. That same rigour is applied to every Vitaprash formulation decision.

The Ayurveda doctor

BAMS · Doctor of Ayurveda

Ayurveda's 3,000-year empirical record is not mysticism — it is the accumulated clinical observation of generations of physicians. Understanding it allows us to ask better questions of modern research.
